Where to buy & get help
What does That BARR do?

That BARR is a handle and holder for compatible pain-relief roll-on bottles. It helps you reach areas such as your back and shoulders. It is an application accessory; the roll-on product supplies the topical treatment.

Will my roll-on bottle fit?

That BARR is designed for most 2.5–3 oz roll-on bottles. Bottle shape matters, so size alone does not guarantee compatibility. Use the bottle-fit guide, check your exact bottle against the product listing, and make sure the bottle sits securely before use.

Does it come with a pain-relief roll-on?

Do not assume a bottle shown in a demonstration is included. Check the current Amazon listing for the exact package contents before ordering.

How do I use it?

Slide a compatible bottle into the holder, remove its cap, and use the handle to apply the roll-on to the desired area. Always follow the directions on your roll-on product and the That BARR instructions.

What is it made of?

That BARR has a one-piece polypropylene body with an I-beam design, a curved handle, and rounded finger grooves. Its published overall length is nearly 18 inches.

Where can I buy it?

Order through the Amazon listing for current pricing and availability. Local purchasing is also listed at Swier Chiropractic, 701 Mid Rivers Mall Drive in Saint Peters, Missouri. Confirm local availability before visiting.

What about shipping, returns, or an existing order?

For an Amazon purchase, check the policies shown with the offer and manage the order through your Amazon account. Contact the seller through Amazon for order-specific help. Purchases made locally should be handled with the clinic.
