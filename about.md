I'm Ken. **Lifelongnerd** is where I keep my field notes — the running log of
things I've built, broken, fixed, and occasionally understood.

## What lands here

- **Build logs.** Projects from first commit to "well, it runs."
- **Post-mortems.** The bugs that cost me a weekend, written up so they only cost you an hour.
- **Tooling.** Editors, terminals, and the small automations that compound.
- **Ideas in progress.** Half-formed thoughts, published anyway.

## The rules

> Write it down while it's still confusing. That's when the notes are worth something.

No listicles, no engagement bait, no AI slop. Just the thing I actually did and
what I actually learned doing it.
